Pofuduk · Coop Adventure

GameIn development

Pofuduk is a mobile-first puzzle adventure sharing one game engine across Android and the web. Slide until an obstacle stops you, collect three chicks, and reach the coop. Water, currents, mushrooms, gates, and teleporters combine across 200 levels in 10 worlds.
