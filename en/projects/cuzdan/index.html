Cüzdan

Cüzdan is a personal finance app designed to go beyond simple expense logging and help users understand where their money goes. It focuses on category-based analysis, charts, and clean mobile flows to improve financial awareness.

How I built it

01

Problem

Many finance apps record numbers but do not make spending habits easy to understand. The goal was to turn expense data into something readable and actionable.

02

My contribution

I designed and built the mobile UI, Firebase-backed data structure, category analysis flows, and chart-based visualizations.

03

Outcome

The app provides a more visual and intuitive finance tracking experience that helps users understand their spending behavior, not just list expenses.
