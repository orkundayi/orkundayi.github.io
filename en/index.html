It begins with curiosity.
It grows through creation.

Alongside Firebase, REST API, SQL, and .NET experience, I use Flame to build game loops, physics, collision systems, and scene management. I shipped FluPass, Cüzdan, and Foxy: Balance of the Glow to mobile stores.
